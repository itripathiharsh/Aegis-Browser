How it works

Ads never reach your screen.
They're stopped at the door.

Most "ad blockers" hide ads after your phone has already downloaded them. Nivrok checks every single network request first — and drops the junk before a byte is spent on it.

  1. 01

    A page asks for 140 things

    A typical news site loads scripts, images and frames from dozens of domains. Many of them exist only to sell your attention.

  2. 02

    Every request hits the filter

    A native domain index checks each request against 52,000+ rules from EasyList, EasyPrivacy and uBlock Origin — in microseconds.

  3. 03

    Junk gets an empty reply

    Blocked requests get nothing back. Leftover ad slots and video overlays are tidied away, and pop-ups never get a window to open in.

Download

Latest release on GitHub · Android 7.0+

  1. Open the latest releaseTap the button and find the .apk file under Assets.
